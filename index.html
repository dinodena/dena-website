Hi, I'm Dena.

I am an engineer who enjoys turning complex mechanical problems into clear, thoughtful, and robust physical and digital experiences. My perspective is shaped by hands-on engineering across aerospace, energy, and consumer tech.

What bridges NASA fluid dynamics and Pixel Buds audio?

At their core, both require an obsession with how invisible forces (air, sound, heat) interact with physical constraints. Whether I am analyzing vehicle purge systems or acoustic resonance in a chassis, the foundational physics and systems-thinking remain identical.

Why does an engineer care so much about music?

Engineering is often highly structured and mathematical. Music provides the creative, unstructured counterbalance. However, producing a great track and engineering a reliable product both rely on harmony, iteration, and fine-tuning small details until the overall system feels right.

What is your design philosophy?

Intuition, craft, and strategy. I believe the best engineering doesn't just work—it feels effortless to the end user. I strive to build systems that are as aesthetically thoughtful as they are technically sound.
